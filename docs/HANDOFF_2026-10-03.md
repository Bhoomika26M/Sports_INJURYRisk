## What to do next, in order of value

1. **Collect clean footage you have the right to use**: ≥30 side-on, single-person squats from ≥10 different people (then other movements).
   That closes the baseline floor, takes false alarms from ~10% to ~2%, and is the single biggest lever. Set `MIN_BASELINE_VIDEOS=30`.
2. **Make squat variant (bodyweight / goblet / barbell) and camera angle baseline dimensions.** Today a barbell back squat reads "moderate" because it differs from a mostly bodyweight population.
3. **Log real injury outcomes** (even a small prospective record). It is the only route to an honest probability, accuracy or false-positive rate;
   until then the product reports risk *levels* with named drivers and must not claim prediction.
4. Investigate `squat_proper_form` (one person, refused at 66% tracking vs a 70% floor): possible over-rejection of multi-person-looking frames.
5. Not yet exercised: `docker compose up`, real Google OAuth, S3/R2 (still local disk), the upload *form* in a browser, and the 56 older browser checks (`frontend/e2e/part1.mjs`, `part2.mjs`).

## Environment notes

- `backend/requirements.txt` pins `mediapipe>=1.0.1`, which bundles **no** pose model. Where Google's model host is unreachable, `mediapipe==0.10.21` (legacy `mp.solutions.pose`, models bundled in the wheel) works and the pipeline supports it.
- YOLO weights (`yolov8n-pose.pt`) are downloaded by ultralytics on first use (a GitHub release asset); they are not in this archive.
