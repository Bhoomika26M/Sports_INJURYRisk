## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite biomechanical telemetry and performance tracking interface. It balances laboratory-grade analytical rigor with real-time pitch-side and court-side legibility.

### Personality & Tone
- **Precision Engineering:** Analytical, quantitative, and uncompromising on scientific credibility.
- **Urgent Clarity:** Instantaneous signal detection under high cognitive load (benchside, field assessments, rehab rooms).
- **Kinetic Dynamism:** Focused on motion, trajectory, force vectors, and physiological resilience without superficial decorative clutter.

### Target Audience
- **Elite Performance Teams:** Biomechanists, sports scientists, athletic performance directors, physiotherapists, head coaches, and high-performance athletes requiring instant risk triangulation and load monitoring.

### Visual Style
- **High-Density Technical Minimalist with HUD-Inspired Utility:** Clean, systematic data architecture built upon precise grid alignments, low-profile ghost strokes, high-contrast metric badges, and clear chromatic risk categorizations.
- **Surface Strategy:** Clinical canvas whites and crisp structural layers framed by 1px muted slate borders, deliberately avoiding saturated decorative backgrounds to let risk signals command immediate visual priority.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unambiguous hierarchy between operational telemetry and physiological risk assessment.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary Brand (`#BD4006`):** Burnt Athletic Ember. Reserved for high-value user triggers, active telemetry stream controls, primary CTAs, and selected spatial tracking nodes.
- **Neutral Primary (`#111827`):** Deep Athletic Slate. Used for primary telemetry readouts, high-contrast typography, and primary metric counts.
- **Canvas (`#F9FAFB`) & Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity, non-fatiguing light base with zero color casting, ensuring screen readability across field tablets and laboratory monitors.
- **Subtle Structural Border (`#E5E7EB`):** Demarcates charts, micro-cards, telemetry streams, and vector feeds.

### Biomechanical Risk & Telemetry Feedback
- **Optimal / Safe (`#10B981` - Emerald):** Normal kinematic envelopes, symmetry scores >92%, balanced landing force.
- **Moderate / Cautionary (`#F59E0B` - Amber):** Kinetic asymmetry (8–14%), acute-to-chronic workload ratio (ACWR) drift, joint angle deviation.
- **Critical Risk (`#EF4444` - Crimson Red):** Elevated injury probability, valgus collapse indicators, tissue strain threshold exceeded, immediate substitution/deload triggers.

### Role Indicator Tags
- **Athlete:** `#1C1613` (Dark Iron Slate)
- **Coach:** `#059669` (Deep Field Emerald)
- **Physiotherapist:** `#7C3AED` (Clinical Violet)
- **Sports Scientist:** `#D97706` (Biomechanical Ochre)
- **Administrator:** `#DC2626` (System Operational Red)

## Typography

Typography acts as a dynamic instrumentation HUD. 

- **Display & Section Headers (Space Grotesk):** Provides geometric precision with distinct athletic-technical angles. Used exclusively for high-level module titles, real-time kinetic indexes, and primary data summaries.
- **Narrative & System Interface (Inter):** Ensures neutral clarity for diagnostic summaries, protocol checklists, physiological descriptions, and input controls.
- **Instrumentation & Telemetry Labels (JetBrains Mono):** Dedicated to coordinate readings, joint angle metrics, timestamps, sensor battery statuses, force readings (N/kg), and confidence interval metrics. Always rendered with tabular alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column fluid grid system engineered for modular telemetry density.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout; gutter `1rem`; margin `1rem`. Side-by-side metric tiles collapse into single-column vertical feeds. Video pose estimation panels drop to a pinned top-dock with metrics scrolling beneath.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout; gutter `1.25rem`; margin `1.5rem`. Split-screen mode: live kinematic visualizer occupies 5 columns, risk vectors and athlete rosters occupy 3 columns.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fixed-fluid layout; gutter `1.5rem`; margin `2.5rem`. Max canvas constraint capped at 1680px for peripheral visual control during match monitoring.

### Rhythmic Density
Telemetry micro-cards and stream units utilize tight internal spacing (`space-sm` and `space-md`) to optimize data visibility above the fold, while inter-module margins (`space-xl`) maintain clear cognitive boundaries between musculoskeletal zones.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain high analytical precision and eliminate visual distraction, this system uses low-contrast outlines combined with shallow structural layering.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#F9FAFB`. Recessed viewport for auxiliary historical tables and background data feeds.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Tier):** Background tone `#FFFFFF`, bordered with a crisp `1px solid #E5E7EB`. Shadow is subtle and neutral: `0px 1px 3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Telemetry Focus & Flyouts):** Elevated HUD overlays, joint angle inspection popovers, and active camera control panels. Border `1px solid #D1D5DB`, shadow `0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Emergency Modals & Critical Deload Alerts):** Modals announcing acute tendon/ligament rupture risk or immediate withdrawal recommendations. Backed by a neutral `#111827` 40% opacity barrier, elevated with `0px 20px 24px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on a **Soft (Level 1)** profile, conveying calculated precision without brutalist sharp hazards.

- **Default Radii (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to telemetry status tags, input fields, checkboxes, chart bars, and table cell badges.
- **Component Radii (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to metric cards, video overlay viewports, diagnostic sheets, and action buttons.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Only physiological status indicators (e.g., "LIVE SENSOR FEED", "OPTIMAL") may use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to maintain clear visual separation from rectangular data fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#BD4006` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`, 8px padding (`0.5rem 1rem`). On hover: `#A33605`. Focus rings: 2px offset with `#BD4006`.
- **Diagnostic / Secondary:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `#111827`. Hover state transitions background to `#F9FAFB` with border `#D1D5DB`.
- **Destructive / Abort:** Solid `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#DC2626`.

### Risk & Telemetry Badges
- Compact inline pills with `0.25rem` radius or pill radius, `JetBrains Mono` 10px uppercase type, paired with an illuminated 6px status dot.
- **Optimal:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, `#10B981` dot.
- **Moderate:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#92400E` text, `#F59E0B` dot.
- **Critical:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#991B1B` text, `#EF4444` dot.

### Biomechanical Micro-Cards
- `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a `1px solid #E5E7EB` stroke.
- Header contains anatomical sub-system title (`Space Grotesk`, 12px, muted `#6B7280`) paired with a monospace risk value.
- Primary metric set in `Space Grotesk` 24px/700, followed by dynamic baseline delta indicators (`+4.2% vs baseline`).

### Kinematic Data Input & Form Controls
- Inputs use `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #E5E7EB` borders, standard 8px internal vertical padding, and `#111827` body text. Active focus states transition border to `#BD4006` with no heavy box-shadow.

### Biomechanical HUD Charts
- Vector axes and reference guide curves rendered in `#E5E7EB`.
- Safe kinematic threshold corridors rendered as semi-transparent `#10B981` bands (8% opacity).
- Active trajectory plotted via 2px solid `#BD4006` line; critical peak force breaches highlighted with `#EF4444` point rings.
